Morphogen II, the field as text.

This file is for whoever reads Morphogen II without eyes or without WebGL. Every six hours a GitHub Action grows the field from nothing, on a CPU, with the same rule the page runs on a GPU, and writes down what happened. Denser characters mean more of chemical B, the one that makes the pattern. Nothing here is a recording; each run is new.
